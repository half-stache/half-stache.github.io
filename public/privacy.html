LavaRise Privacy Policy

Last updated: February 22, 2026

This privacy policy describes how LavaRise ("the App", "we", "our") collects, uses, and shares information when you use our mobile game.

Information We Collect

Information You Provide

  • In-App Purchases: Purchase transactions are processed by Apple through the App Store. We do not collect or store payment information.

Information Collected Automatically

  • Game Progress: Your game progress, settings, and preferences are stored locally on your device. This data is not transmitted to any server.
  • Advertising Data: We use Google AdMob to display ads. AdMob may collect device identifiers, IP address, and usage data to serve ads. See Google's Privacy Policy for details.
  • Ad Performance: We receive aggregated, anonymous reports on ad impressions and interactions. These reports do not identify individual users.

Advertising

LavaRise displays ads provided by Google AdMob. You can choose whether ads are personalized based on your interests or non-personalized. This choice is presented when you first open the app and can be changed in Settings.

If you opt into personalized ads, AdMob may use your advertising identifier and browsing data to show relevant ads. If you decline, you will still see ads, but they will not be tailored to your interests.

On iOS, we request App Tracking Transparency (ATT) authorization before any cross-app tracking occurs. You can change this at any time in your device's Settings.

Third-Party Services

  • Google AdMob: Ad serving and mediation. Privacy Policy
  • Apple App Store: App distribution and in-app purchases. Privacy Policy
  • SKAdNetwork: Apple's privacy-preserving ad attribution framework. No personal data is shared.

Data Storage

All game data (progress, settings, achievements, daily rewards) is stored locally on your device using the app's sandboxed storage. We do not operate servers and do not transmit your game data off your device.

Children's Privacy

LavaRise is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. AdMob is configured to comply with applicable regulations regarding ads shown to users.

Your Choices

  • Personalized Ads: You can opt out of personalized ads in the app's Settings or through your device's privacy settings.
  • App Tracking: On iOS, you can manage tracking permissions in Settings > Privacy > Tracking.
  • Reset Advertising Identifier: You can reset your advertising identifier in your device's privacy settings.
  • Delete Data: Uninstalling the app removes all locally stored game data.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. The updated policy will be posted at this URL with a revised "Last updated" date.
