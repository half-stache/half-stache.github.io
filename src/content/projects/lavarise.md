## What it is

LavaRise is a mobile game for iOS. It is free to play, supported by ads, with optional in-app purchases.

## Built to respect the player

- All game progress, settings, achievements, and daily rewards are stored on the device. There is no server and no account.
- Ads are served through Google AdMob. The player chooses personalized or non-personalized ads on first launch and can change the choice in Settings.
- App Tracking Transparency authorization is requested before any cross-app tracking, and SKAdNetwork handles attribution without sharing personal data.
- Purchases go through the App Store. The app never sees payment details.
- Uninstalling removes everything.
