## What it is

Hutch's RV Park sits across from the White County Fairgrounds in Searcy, Arkansas: full hookups, a new shower building, pickleball and basketball courts, and the Searcy bike and walking trail at the gate. The site has to do three things well. Answer the questions a traveler asks before booking, stay current when deals and events change, and look like the place.

## What was built

- A Next.js site with structured content for accommodations, facilities, attractions, nearby places, events, deals, FAQs, and a photo gallery.
- Keystatic as a Git-backed CMS, so the owner edits text, photos, deals, and events in a plain editor and the site rebuilds itself. No database to maintain, no monthly CMS bill.
- The brand mark, app icons, and the site's visual language.
- Sitemap, robots, and page metadata generated in code, so search engines see the right thing without anyone remembering to update it.
- A seam for Riverstock, the sister app for the Little Red River a few minutes north of the park, ready to switch on when the app ships.

## The hard part

Making a content system a non-technical owner will actually use. Every field in the editor is one the owner recognizes from running the park, and nothing requires a developer to change.
